The concept

NOVA is an imaginary electric-citrus drink built to feel like a burst of light. The campaign starts with a simple idea: every sip is a tiny supernova.

  • Identity. A star-in-a-ring mark with a purple, orange and lime gradient that glows against black.
  • Web. A one-page launch site with oversized type and one clear action: shop.
  • Film. A fast, high-contrast 30-second promo cut to a heartbeat bass line.
